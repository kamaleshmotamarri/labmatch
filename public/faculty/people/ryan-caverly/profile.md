# Ryan Caverly

![Ryan Caverly](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Associate Professor, Distinguished University Teaching Professor

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** rcaverly@umn.edu

## Research summary

Caverly develops mathematical models and control methods for aircraft, spacecraft, and robots. He especially studies flexible structures and how to control their motion accurately, efficiently, and reliably.
