# Suraj Ravindran

![Suraj Ravindran](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Assistant Professor

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** sravi@umn.edu

## Research summary

Ravindran studies how materials behave under extreme pressures, temperatures, and rapid deformation, as encountered in high-speed flight and protective systems. He combines impact experiments, fast imaging, and computational tools to examine materials such as ceramics, glass, alloys, composites, and polymers across many scales.
