# Graham V. Candler

![Graham V. Candler](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Associate Department Head, Professor and McKnight Presidential Endowed Chair

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** candler@umn.edu

## Research summary

Candler uses computer simulations to study extremely fast, hot gas flows and the chemical reactions within them. His work helps analyze spacecraft heat shields, the onset of turbulence, and air inlets for hypersonic aircraft.

## Easy research quiz

3. What spacecraft component does his work help analyze?

## Answer key

1. Computer simulations.
2. Very fast and hot.
3. Heat shields.
4. Yes.
5. Hypersonic aircraft.
