# Ellad B. Tadmor

![Ellad B. Tadmor](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Professor, Russell J. Penrose Professor

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** tadmor@umn.edu

## Research summary

Tadmor develops physics-based and machine-learning models of how solid and two-dimensional materials respond to heat, forces, and failure. He connects atomic-scale behavior with larger-scale mechanics using molecular dynamics, finite-element methods, and multiscale modeling.
