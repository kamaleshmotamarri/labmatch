# Joseph W. Nichols

![Joseph W. Nichols](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Associate Professor

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** jwn@umn.edu

## Research summary

Nichols uses large computer simulations to study noise from high-speed jets and instability in hypersonic flows. He also develops scalable analysis tools to predict how flows over complex shapes become unstable and transition toward turbulence.
