# Demoz Gebre-Egziabher

![Demoz Gebre-Egziabher](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Professor & Director of the Minnesota Space Grant Consortium

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** gebre@umn.edu

## Research summary

Gebre-Egziabher develops navigation, guidance, and control methods for aerospace vehicles by combining information from multiple sensors. His work emphasizes reliability and supports precise aircraft navigation, satellite orientation measurements, and small autonomous aircraft.

## Easy research quiz

1. What does navigation help a vehicle determine?
2. Does he combine information from one sensor only or multiple sensors?
3. What quality does his research emphasize besides accuracy?
4. What satellite property does his work help measure?
5. Does his research apply to small autonomous aircraft?

## Answer key

1. Where it is and how to reach a destination.
2. Multiple sensors.
3. Reliability.
4. Orientation, also called attitude.
5. Yes.
