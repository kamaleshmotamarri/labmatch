# James Scoggins

![James Scoggins](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Associate Professor

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** jscoggin@umn.edu

## Research summary

Scoggins combines physics-based modeling and machine learning to predict extreme heating and gas-flow behavior during hypersonic flight. His work includes spacecraft and meteor entry, radiation, thermal-protection materials, and interpreting flight sensors under uncertainty.
