# Joseph Mueller

![Joseph Mueller](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Industrial Professor of Design

**Category:** Teaching & Research Faculty

**Email:** muel0053@umn.edu

## Research summary

Mueller studies control, navigation, estimation, and path planning for aerospace vehicles. His interests include satellite constellations, space-mission design, autonomous aircraft routing, and algorithms that help spacecraft approach and operate near one another.
