# Kshitiz Upadhyay

![Kshitiz Upadhyay](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Assistant Professor

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** kshitizu@umn.edu

## Research summary

Upadhyay studies how soft materials such as biological tissues and polymers deform, heat up, and suffer damage under demanding conditions. He combines advanced experiments and physics-informed machine learning for applications including brain injury, protective systems, and discovering new materials.
