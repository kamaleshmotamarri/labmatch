# Perry H. Leo

![Perry H. Leo](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Professor

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** phleo@umn.edu

## Research summary

Leo studies how internal patterns and phase changes affect the properties of complex solids. His theoretical and numerical work links microstructure to strength, fracture and fatigue resistance, and electrical or magnetic behavior in materials such as composites, alloys, biological materials, and liquid crystals.
