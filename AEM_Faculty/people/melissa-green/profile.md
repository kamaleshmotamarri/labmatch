# Melissa Green

![Melissa Green](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Associate Professor

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** magreen@umn.edu

## Research summary

Green studies how moving air and water interact with surfaces such as flapping wings and oscillating fins. She investigates and visualizes vortices and other organized flow patterns to understand forces and help predict or control fluid motion.
