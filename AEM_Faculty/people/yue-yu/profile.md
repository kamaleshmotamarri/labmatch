# Yue Yu

![Yue Yu](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Assistant Professor

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** yuey@umn.edu

## Research summary

Yu develops efficient optimization and control algorithms for autonomous aerospace systems. His work includes planning paths in real time and coordinating multiple vehicles using game theory, with applications to advanced air mobility and spacecraft control.
