# Terrence R. Meyer

![Terrence R. Meyer](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Professor & Department Head

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** trmeyer@umn.edu

## Research summary

Meyer develops advanced measurement tools to understand fast, hot, reacting flows for propulsion, power, and energy systems. He studies complex flows containing droplets or particles and works toward devices with better efficiency, lower emissions, and practical designs.
