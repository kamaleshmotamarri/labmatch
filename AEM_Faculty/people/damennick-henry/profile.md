# Damennick Henry

![Damennick Henry](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Assistant Professor

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** henry474@umn.edu

## Research summary

Henry develops mathematical theory and computational tools for spacecraft operating in complex, chaotic orbital environments. His applications include fuel-saving transfers near Earth and the Moon, coordinated spacecraft formations, and guidance, navigation, and control.

## Easy research quiz

4. What resource can better orbital transfers help save?
5. What does formation flying involve?

1. Spacecraft.
2. No; they can be complex and chaotic.

4. Propellant or fuel.
