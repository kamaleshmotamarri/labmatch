# Everett Wenzel

![Everett Wenzel](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Research Associate Professor

**Category:** Teaching & Research Faculty

**Email:** eawenzel@umn.edu

## Research summary

Wenzel develops numerical methods and scientific software for flows involving multiple phases, such as sprays and smoke aerosols. He studies phase boundaries, their curvature, phase-change rates, and interactions between shock waves and droplets.
