# James A. Flaten

**Category:** Teaching & Research Faculty

## Research summary

Flaten leads hands-on NASA-related education and projects involving high-altitude balloons, miniature spacecraft, rocket payloads, and student rocketry. His academic background is experimental low-temperature physics, and his outreach connects physics with sound, art, and engaging demonstrations.
