# Tom Schwartzentruber

![Tom Schwartzentruber](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Professor, Distinguished McKnight University Professor

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** schwart@umn.edu

## Research summary

Schwartzentruber uses particle-based computer simulations to study gases that are chemically reacting or out of equilibrium. His work combines molecular and larger-scale models for applications including high-altitude hypersonic flight, rocket plumes, gas-surface interactions, and tiny devices.
