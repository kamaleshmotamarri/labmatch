**Category:** Faculty

## Research summary

Hemati develops ways to model, understand, and control fluid flows using theory, computer simulations, and experiments. Applications include reducing drag by delaying turbulence, combining sensor measurements, controlling flow separation, and improving hypersonic flight systems.
