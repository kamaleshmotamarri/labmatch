# Ryan S. Elliott

![Ryan S. Elliott](photo.jpg)

**Role:** Professor & Director of Graduate Studies

**Category:** Faculty

**Email:** relliott@umn.edu

## Research summary

Elliott studies why structures and materials become unstable, including buckling, aircraft-wing flutter, and structural collapse. He develops nonlinear mathematical models, computational methods, and open-source software to predict instability and discover different stable configurations.
